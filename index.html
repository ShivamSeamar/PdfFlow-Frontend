Why it's safe to use

Privacy-first, by construction.

I.

Nothing is uploaded

Your PDFs are read and rewritten entirely inside this browser tab. No file ever touches a server, ours or anyone else's.

II.

Works without a connection

Once the page has loaded, split, extract and compress all keep working offline — there's simply nothing to send.

III.

Closing the tab clears everything

Nothing is written to disk or stored anywhere. Refresh the page and every file, thumbnail and selection is gone for good.
